## What I built

Research projects often need a public home: somewhere to explain the work, recruit survey respondents or collect feedback. I've built these for:

- **First Nations Health Authority (2024).** A project web page and feedback form for the Evaluation of FNHA and the BC Tripartite Framework Agreement.
- **Vancouver Police Department and Vancouver School Board (2024 – 2025).** A project web page and survey access for the School Liaison Officer Program Evaluation.
- **Lunenburg Waterfront Association (2025 – 2026).** A community engagement website for the Lunenburg Waterfront Strategic Plan.
- **Western Forestry Contractors Association (2021).** A survey recruitment page for the Silviculture Labour Market Strategy.
- **Town of Fort Smith, NWT.** An interactive community data profile.
