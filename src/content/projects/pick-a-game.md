## The idea

I own about 140 board games, which makes figuring out what to play on game night a bit of a challenge. This site helps narrow down the options, filtering through the board games I own.
## How it works

- **Answer a few questions.** How many players, how much time you have, how much thinking you're up for, and what kind of game fits the mood.
- **Get a suggestion, with backups.** It suggests a game for tonight, plus a few other good choices.
- **The whole shelf.** It's pulls data from my BoardGameGeek collection to know what games I own and details about each game.
