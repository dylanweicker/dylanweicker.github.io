## The problem

The Chefs' Table Society's Sector Labour Market Partnership project helped BC restaurant and foodservice employers improve workplace culture and keep their staff, and Qatalyst evaluated it. Busy operators needed advice that fit their own restaurant, and a way to keep getting help after the project ended.

## What I built

- **An eight-section questionnaire** on recruitment, retention, training, culture and operations.
- **A restaurant profile and an AI-generated roadmap** across seven HR areas, linked to templates from the project's toolkit.
- **A chatbot** that answers operators' questions from the project's resources.
- **Admin tools** to build surveys, view charted results and export responses.

## The results

The project reached 101 employers, and 92% were satisfied with the help they received. The platform still gives operators self-directed support now that the project is over.

## Under the hood

Nuxt and Vuetify on Firebase. The roadmap and the retrieval-augmented chatbot run in Cloud Functions, and administrators manage the chatbot's resources themselves.
