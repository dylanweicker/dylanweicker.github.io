## The project

Economic development officers from seven communities engaged Qatalyst to attract technology investment to the mid-Island corridor, with a focus on clean tech and the circular economy. The communities were Campbell River, Port Alberni, Nanaimo, Parksville, Qualicum Beach, the Cowichan Valley Regional District and the Regional District of Mount Waddington. Qatalyst delivered an action plan, promotional materials, a business case and investment video, and a social media campaign aimed at the western United States.

## What I built

- **An updated techisland.io** that includes the new member municipalities.
- **An interactive cluster map** of the region's technology businesses.
