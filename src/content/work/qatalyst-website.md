## What I built

I built and maintain qatalyst.ca. Its admin content system manages the blog, case studies, webinars, jobs, and staff and associate profiles. Associate profiles are drawn from Qatalink, so they're kept up to date in one place.

## 2026 redesign

In 2026 I redesigned the site and strengthened its security. I added structured data, clean URLs and faster image delivery to improve search rankings, and set up automatic deployment.
